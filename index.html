Kitted is the new UK marketplace for club communities to buy and sell pre-loved sports kit and equipment. Outgrown boots, pads, sticks and club shirts go to someone who'll use them, and every sale helps your club.

Supports the club

Every sale gives at least 2% to your club. Sellers can choose to give more.

Keeps kit in play

Pre-loved kit gets another season instead of sitting in a cupboard.

Sport for everyone

Good kit at fair prices, so cost isn't a barrier to playing.

How it works

  1. 1

    Join your club

    Sign up and join your club's community on Kitted.

  2. 2

    List or find kit

    Sell the kit you've outgrown and pick up quality gear for less.

  3. 3

    Your club benefits

    Part of every sale goes to your club, and sellers can choose to give more.
